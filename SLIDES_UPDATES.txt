Bottom: 3-bullet explainer (replace current side text):

  1. WHY IT'S A WIN
     Merge-path partitions by NNZ count but cannot split a hub row across
     threads. One thread becomes a straggler; others wait at the barrier.

  2. HOW HYBRID FIXES IT
     Inspector slices hub rows into 1024-NNZ chunks. All threads pull from
     a shared queue. By construction, no thread can be more than one chunk
     ahead of another.

  3. WHY GFLOPs DOESN'T SHOW THE FULL WIN
     Both methods saturate DRAM at 8+ threads on hollywood. Total
     throughput is hardware-capped. Hybrid uses that bandwidth more
     equitably across threads — fairness, not throughput.
